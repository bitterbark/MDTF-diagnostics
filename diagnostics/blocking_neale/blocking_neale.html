Blocking Diagnostic (Rich Neale)

The diagnostic evaluates the blocking frequency in a model by longitude and season as determined by the meridional gradient above a threshold value of daily 500mb height following D'Andrea et al 1998.

The annual cycle of blocking frequency is shown for each analyzed case, the reference ensemble (CAM5) and the observational products (ERAI & MERRA) requested, in individual Hovmüller figures (longitude vs time). All cases and references are combined in a line plot of blocking frequency vs longitude. Each figure is currently shown for ANN.
